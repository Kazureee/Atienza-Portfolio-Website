How I work

A clear process, so you know what happens at every stage.

  1. 1

    Map the process

    I learn how the task is done today, where time is lost and which systems are involved.

  2. 2

    Design the workflow

    I plan the triggers, steps and error handling, and confirm the plan with you before building.

  3. 3

    Build and test

    I build in the right tool and test with real cases, including what happens when something fails.

  4. 4

    Hand over

    You get an SOP, a Loom walkthrough and training, so your team can run and maintain it.
